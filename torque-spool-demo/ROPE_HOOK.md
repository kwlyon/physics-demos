# Low-profile rope hook

The accessory is a thread-and-rotate J hook for the recessed axial rope post. Its tapered 0.65 mm leading tip passes through the measured 0.90 mm entry beside the post; rotating the part seats the J around the post and places the long tail tangent to the spool. The tail is only 1.8 mm thick radially. A raised, tapered eyelet at the trailing end has a smooth 5.2 mm clear opening for tying common 2–3 mm demonstration cord.

Two STLs are supplied because the thinnest insert cannot carry the same post diameter as the others:

- `Low_Profile_Rope_Hook_6p2mm_Post.stl` fits the original spool and the 30°, 40°, 45°, and 50° insert posts.
- `Low_Profile_Rope_Hook_4p0mm_Post.stl` fits the reduced post on the 60° insert.

## Installation

1. Feed the tapered hook nose into the narrow opening beside the post.
2. Slide and rotate the J profile around the post; it is not intended to snap over the post.
3. Swing the rounded tail back until its inner face lies against the winding surface.
4. Pass the rope through the raised eyelet, tie a compact knot, and begin winding in the direction that keeps the hook tail pulled flat.

Print the hook in PETG, nylon, or another tough material. Use 0.16–0.20 mm layers, at least four walls, and 100% infill because the part is small. Place one broad axial face on the build plate. A 0.25 mm radial clearance is built around each target post.

The meshes pass software checks for watertightness, consistent winding, one connected body, positive volume, entry-tip clearance, and eye opening. A physical test print is still required; the 0.65 mm tapered nose is deliberately thin so it can negotiate the measured 0.90 mm entry.
